1. What the Software is1. Что такое Программа

mpthree-dl is a free, open-source desktop application that runs on your own computer. It combines metadata from third-party catalogs and links with third-party tools (yt-dlp and FFmpeg) to find, download and tag audio that you ask for. The project's repository and this website contain source code, installers and documentation; they do not contain or host music, video or other media. Downloads are started by you, run on your device, and fetch content from third-party sources.

Оно объединяет метаданные сторонних каталогов и ссылки со сторонними инструментами (yt-dlp и FFmpeg), чтобы находить, скачивать и тегировать запрошенное вами аудио.
